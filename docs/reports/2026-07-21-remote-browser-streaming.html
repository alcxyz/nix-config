Defect register

Known visual qualification

△ D-01 — Brave motion and UI glitching

Severity: experience-affecting, non-blocking · Affects: Brave

Animated page elements, scrolling chat, address-bar interaction, and occasionally the playing video showed visible stutter or graphical glitches. Wolf, NVENC, Moonlight, and resource telemetry stayed healthy, so this qualifies Brave's application result rather than failing the transport.

Method

Like-for-like sustained playback

Test procedure and acceptance conditions
  1. Launch one isolated browser runner through the protected Wolf selector.
  2. Open the same YouTube test video, clear consent or advertisements, and explicitly select 1440p.
  3. Play actual footage for at least two minutes through a 2560×1440/60 HEVC stream at 40 Mbit/s.
  4. Sample browser container CPU/memory, Wolf CPU, NVENC use, XPS Moonlight CPU/RSS, and thermals.
  5. Scan browser, Wolf, and Moonlight logs for crashes, encoder/decoder faults, drops, or timeouts.
Interpretation boundary

Resource and log telemetry can qualify transport stability, but it does not measure every rendered-frame defect. Subjective visual observations therefore remain evidence alongside the measurements rather than being overwritten by them.

Limits

What this report does not claim

  • The ranges are comparative observations from one controlled campaign, not universal browser benchmarks.
  • Advertisements and consent screens were excluded from qualified timing windows.
  • Background Moonlight clients still decode and consume network/thermal budget until explicitly closed.
  • Clipboard, Synergy pointer feel, and KDE Connect pointer behavior were outside this playback acceptance run.

Next
